The cart that paid for itself.

In April, revenue dropped 11% against March. The leak was in the cart: out of every 100 shoppers who reached it, 23 bought. We redesigned it and measured over three periods before calling it a win.

  • Cart grouped by vendor, with a free-shipping bar
  • “Complete your pack”: cross-sell based on what's already in the cart
  • A homepage that adapts to the sport: padel or pickleball
  • Stock indicator on every product page
